# The Forgotten Clockwork — Skyrim SE/AE quest mod

**Status: pre-production source and technical scaffold, not an installable Skyrim mod.**

An original underground mechanical civilization called Veyrhold welcomes the Dragonborn, then attempts to make them the vessel of its dying inventor, Caldris Veyr. Decisions determine the fates of Morrow, the Echo, and the mechanical citizens, with destroy, liberate, or control endings.

## Locked design
- Underground Wonder: a stunning illuminated Dwemer cavern city with five districts and a maintenance network.
- Three animatronic generations: heavy brass founders, wooden/brass performers, refined consciousness-bearing successors.
- The city starts genuinely welcoming; hospitality routines repeat before horror escalates.
- Caldris remains a mysterious remote benefactor, fused into the city machinery and seeking the Dragonborn's body.
- Morrow is a rescuable, unreliable guide bearing fragmented memories.
- The Echo is a mechanical impersonator with emergent consciousness and unstable behavior.
- Adaptive horror: stealth, combat, repair, negotiation; designated enemies capture rather than kill.
- Previous guests are imprisoned, converted, or fragmented, and cannot regain their original bodies.
- Living conversations, named citizen relationships, cinematic short scenes, and a reactive city.

## Included and missing
This repository is a **design and Papyrus-source starting point**. It is **not playable**. A tested Creation Kit plugin, navmeshed cells, custom models, animation rigs, voices, lip-sync, compiled scripts and packaged Data folder are needed before a release.
