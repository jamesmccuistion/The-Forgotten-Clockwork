## Visual rules
Ancient constructs: brass and exposed iron, industrial geometry, heavy motions. Theater generation: wood masks, fabric, leather, visible brass understructure. Successors: refined anatomical proportions and concealed mechanisms, but *never* modern plastics or electronics. All finished characters require custom, licensed assets or demonstrably permitted sources.

## Voice rules
Expressive, individually characterized voices, recorded/cleared for game redistribution. No actor imitation without permission. Voices bundled offline. Every important line also has readable subtitles. Dialogue variants must be gated on stable quest flags.

## Relationship logic
Not every actor needs five variables; use Trust, Fear, Loyalty, Independence, MemoryIntegrity selectively. Never allow scores alone to bypass mandatory quest milestones.
