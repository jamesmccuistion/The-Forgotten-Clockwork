# Prototype 01 dialogue and letter

## The invitation — original book content
To the traveler whose deeds have reached even our quiet halls:

There exists beneath the mountains a city seldom visited and never properly understood. Its people are craftsmen, performers, guardians, and scholars. We ask only that you come as our guest, and that you judge us by what you see rather than what is said of us.

Follow the enclosed bearings to the sealed brass approach. Present this letter. A room and a place at our table await you.

With sincere admiration,
The Benefactor of Veyrhold

## Gatekeeper
Before letter: "The city is not open to unannounced travelers."
With letter: "Ah. The honored guest. You have come farther than most. Welcome to Veyrhold."
Player: "What is this place?"
Gatekeeper: "A refuge for craft, memory, and useful hands. The steward can tell you more."
Player: "May I leave again?"
Gatekeeper: "Of course. We have no wish to keep a guest against their will." [true in Act I]
Gatekeeper accepts: "The Master regrets he cannot greet you in person. Please, follow the lanterns."

## First welcome
Steward: "Welcome to Veyrhold. We do hope you'll make yourself at home."
Steward: "There is a room prepared for you. But first, perhaps a stroll through our market?"
Player: "You were expecting me?"
Steward: "Expecting is such a serious word. We were simply ready."
